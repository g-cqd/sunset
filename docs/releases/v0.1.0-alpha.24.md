- **Files open drawn**: HTML, XHTML and SVG files are drawn as pages, redrawn as you edit their source; they read only
  their folder and stay offline unless you allow it. PDFs, pictures, sounds and films open in the system's viewers.
- **Opening files**: a terminal's file links and downloads open in Sunset what it can show. Documents › Open files
  chooses drawn or as source.
- **Light and dark**: a System | Light | Dark switch over the look settings, with a preview; System shows macOS's values.
  The terminal's colors are in Look and feel.
- **Updates**: an update that could not be put in place is said at the next launch, with the reason; Restart to Update
  opens Sunset again either way.
