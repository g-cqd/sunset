The fifth alpha of Sunset, in two builds. Expect rough edges, and settings or saved state that a later build may not read.

**Two builds.**
- **Sunset** (`Sunset-v0.1.0-alpha.5-…`): pages run on the system's WebKit, the one Safari uses, and get its security updates
  with macOS. Most people want this one.
- **Sunset WebKit** (`Sunset-WebKit-v0.1.0-alpha.5-…`, experimental): the same app with its own WebKit inside, built from
  WebKit's main branch (aa9b09cf67) with 37 patches of ours: browser extensions written for Firefox and Chrome (uBlock
  Origin among them), pages that were orders of magnitude slower than in other browsers, and one crash fixed upstream the
  day after that commit. Read before using it:
  - it gets **no WebKit security fixes from macOS updates**, only from a later Sunset WebKit;
  - to load its own WebKit it runs with a loader exception (`allow-dyld-environment-variables`) that the plain build does
    not have, and without the private entitlements Safari's WebKit has for its sandbox; its threat model is not written yet;
  - it is a separate app (`studio.aemi.sunset.webkit`) with its own settings, beside Sunset if both are installed.

**What changed since alpha.4.**
- uBlock Origin in Sunset WebKit: `$csp` filters, scriptlets on every visit (not only the first), pop-up blocking (`$popup`),
  generic cosmetic filters, its settings page (it showed every box unchecked), its "subscribe to this list" menu item.
- Sunset WebKit, for extensions: response headers (`onHeadersReceived` with `blocking`) for pages and frames, also when they
  come from the cache; events for loads answered from the cache; `webRequest.handlerBehaviorChanged`. What an extension
  answers is checked: a malformed header is dropped instead of failing the page. With uBlock Origin, a page's requests in
  parallel take as long as on the system's WebKit.
- Sunset WebKit, pages: long runs of comments or text, a `Range`'s contents taken out, `normalize()`, a list's children read
  out of order, and `:nth-child()` in `querySelectorAll` now take time in proportion to the page, not its square (40,000
  comments: 1.4 s before, 5 ms now). A crash when a large grid is removed after a page of inline text is fixed.
- Extension buttons show disabled in tabs that are not web pages, and appear in pop-up and peek windows.

**Install.** Unzip, move the app to Applications, open it. Both are signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so
they open without a warning. They need macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Check the download.** `shasum -a 256 -c NAME.zip.sha256` in the folder holding both files.

**Not in it yet.** Linux; Intel Macs; updates (download the next build by hand); in Sunset WebKit, extensions changing request
headers, or the headers of images, scripts and other subresources (pages and frames only); uBlock Origin's privacy switches
(pre-fetching, hyperlink auditing) show but change nothing; several pieces of the roadmap (`docs/roadmap`, each entry says
what is not done).

Report what breaks in the repository's issues.
