The twenty-second alpha of Sunset: updates install again; widening the sidebar in Settings no longer crashes; the
terminal has a light scheme; two-key shortcuts; KeePass and 1Password imports, and a KeePass export. Expect rough edges,
and settings or saved state that a later build may not read.

**What changed since alpha.21.**
- **Updates**: the running app's signature is read as it starts. Before, once its files were replaced on disk while it
  ran, every update was refused as having "no readable signature".
- **Crash**: widening the sidebar while Settings was shown could crash the app. The sidebar's and the panes' sizes are
  stored after the window is laid out, and only when they changed.
- **Terminal in light**: dark text on a near-white screen, with ANSI colours dark enough to read on it, while Sunset is
  light; dark as before while it is dark. Colours you chose apply to both unless you split them. The 16 ANSI colours can
  differ between light and dark, like the other colours.
- **Light and dark on one line** in Settings: a sun and a moon control, and a button to split a setting in two.
- **Two-key shortcuts**: `cmd+k cmd+t` or `cmd+k t` in `keys`. The window says it waits for the second key; Escape or a
  click stops. Settings records two keys.
- **Passwords**:
  - Import from KeePass (KDBX 4) and 1Password (`.1pux`).
  - Export to KeePass (KDBX 4).
  - Fields renamed in place and dragged to reorder.
- **History**: Clear, and its profiles (all, this one, grouped); each visit notes its profile.
- Settings and Help fit the pane they are in.

**Two variants of one app.**
- **Sunset** (`Sunset-v0.1.0-alpha.22-…`): pages on the system's WebKit, the one Safari uses.
- **Sunset with its own WebKit** (`Sunset-WebKit-v0.1.0-alpha.22-…`): the same app with WebKit's main branch at
  `c308255c50` and 37 patches of ours. Switch between them in Settings › General › Updates › Web engine.

**Install.** Unzip, move the app to Applications, open it. Both are signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple.
They need macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Check the download.** `shasum -a 256 -c NAME.zip.sha256` in the folder holding both files.

**Updating from alpha.21 or earlier.** Their updater may refuse this update as having "no readable signature" if the app
was replaced while it ran: quit Sunset, open it again, then update. From alpha.20 or earlier, if Sunset does not quit
after Restart, quit it yourself.

**Not in it yet.** Linux; Intel Macs; syncing the vault between Macs; protected video on some sites.

Report what breaks in the repository's issues.
