The ninth alpha of Sunset, in two builds. Expect rough edges, and settings or saved state that a later build may not read.

**Two builds.**
- **Sunset** (`Sunset-v0.1.0-alpha.9-…`): pages run on the system's WebKit, the one Safari uses, and get its security updates
  with macOS. Most people want this one.
- **Sunset WebKit** (`Sunset-WebKit-v0.1.0-alpha.9-…`, experimental): the same app with its own WebKit inside, built from
  WebKit's main branch at 944f9ff672 with 36 patches of ours. It gets **no WebKit security fixes from macOS updates**, only
  from a later Sunset WebKit; it says when its engine is eight weeks old. It now shares Sunset's own data (see below).

**What changed since alpha.8.**
- **Passwords**, Sunset's own password manager, off until you turn it on (Settings ▸ General ▸ Features, or the welcome):
  - a vault sealed on this Mac, unlocked with Touch ID, a master password, or a recovery code shown once; it locks when the
    Mac sleeps, the screen locks, Sunset quits or its last window closes, and after a while idle;
  - its section in the Library; entries made from templates (Login, Card, Identity, Secure Note, Wi-Fi, SSH Key, and more),
    edited on a page of their own, fields reordered by dragging; a password generator;
  - one-time codes (TOTP, HOTP, Steam): shown with their countdown, set up from an `otpauth://` link, a secret, Google
    Authenticator's export, or a QR code's picture pasted, dropped or opened;
  - in pages: your logins suggested under a sign-in field, filled only when you click; Use Strong Password on sign-up
    forms; codes into code fields; cards after Touch ID; an offer to save or update a password after you sign in;
  - a password copied leaves the clipboard after 90 seconds; the window cannot be captured while the vault is unlocked.
- **Now Playing.** What plays in your pages shows as cards at the foot of the sidebar: play and pause, previous and next,
  mute, picture in picture, back to its tab. Settings ▸ Websites ▸ Media turns either off.
- **The two builds share Sunset's data**: Sunset WebKit uses Sunset's store, settings and key; one runs at a time;
  File ▸ Bring Cookies from Sunset brings your sign-ins over.
- **⌘W** closes the focused tab, not the window; a pinned or favorite tab is unloaded.
- **The sidebar.** The Spaces at its foot turn to dots when there are many (or always, in Settings), the one shown in white;
  the Library's panel fades its rows above its foot; its order button is a button like the others.
- **Tab previews** are taken again when they are half a minute old.
- **The command bar** lines its field up with its rows.

**Install.** Unzip, move the app to Applications, open it. Both are signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so
they open without a warning. They need macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Check the download.** `shasum -a 256 -c NAME.zip.sha256` in the folder holding both files.

**Not in it yet.** Linux; Intel Macs; updates (download the next build by hand); images in the terminal (begun); syncing
the vault between Macs, importing from other password managers, addresses in pages; several pieces of the roadmap
(`docs/roadmap`, each entry says what is not done).

Report what breaks in the repository's issues.
