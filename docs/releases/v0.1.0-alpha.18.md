The eighteenth alpha of Sunset: terminal scrollback search, Keychain-sealed session replay across quit, Sixel & iTerm2
inline images, Kitty animations, VT400 rectangular ops, OSC 21 colour control, and per-style/fallback/variable fonts;
credential vault secret history, sealed attachments, EFF Diceware passphrases, offline Password Health, OTP & Bitwarden
encrypted imports, same-origin iframe & identity autofill, optional fill-on-load, and a concentric fill panel with
locked-vault unlock offers; Markdown **Format** menu commands; sidebar, notification, Now Playing, context-menu, and
screen-sharing polish; and Sunset WebKit updated to `c308255c50`. Expect rough edges, and settings or saved state that a
later build may not read.

**What changed since alpha.17.**
- **Terminal: search, session restore, inline graphics, VT400, shell integration, and typography.**
  - **Scrollback & live-screen search** (`Cmd+F`): plain-text and RE2 regex search across scrollback and the active
    screen with CPU and Metal match highlighting.
  - **Session restore across quit**: terminal screens and scrollback are sealed with a Keychain-wrapped key and replayed
    on launch, with crash-aware launch restore policy.
  - **Inline graphics & animations**: Sixel DCS (`xterm`-conformant sizing/placement), iTerm2 `OSC 1337 File=` (single
    and chunked `MultipartFile=`/`FilePart=` as sent by `imgcat`), and Kitty graphics animations (`a=f`/`a=a`/`a=c`,
    image numbers `I=`, terminal-driven frame gaps, loop counts, and alternate-screen scrolling).
  - **VT400 & colour control**: rectangular area operations (`DECFRA`, `DECERA`, `DECCRA`), reverse wraparound
    (`DECSET 45`), `XTSAVE`/`XTRESTORE` private mode save/restore, and `OSC 21` Kitty dynamic colour queries/changes.
  - **Shell integration & paths**: `bash` command marking (preserving existing `PROMPT_COMMAND` and `bash-preexec`
    arrays) and exact `OSC 7` working-directory reporting; `Cmd`-clicking plain file paths (with optional `:line[:col]`)
    opens Markdown/text files beside the terminal while revealing risky macOS bundle/executable types in Finder instead
    of launching them.
  - **Typography & rendering**: per-style font overrides (family or PostScript face), ordered fallback font cascade,
    variable font axis configuration, and a sound GSUB/`morx`-verified ASCII fast path bypassing CoreText on plain runs.
- **Passwords & autofill.**
  - **Vault capabilities & imports**: previous-value secret history, sealed file attachments, 6-word EFF Short Wordlist
    Diceware passphrases, offline **Password Health** inspection (judging passwords only, not PINs/CVVs/keys), HTTP
    autofill warnings, atomic single-transaction batch imports, CSV note/card imports, Bitwarden password-protected JSON
    decryption (PBKDF2-SHA256 & Argon2id), Aegis / 2FAS / andOTP JSON imports, and HOTP counter advancement on fill.
  - **Autofill across fields, frames & load**: vault identities are offered on contact and address fields; credentials,
    payment cards, and identities are offered and filled inside same-origin `<iframe>`s; optional automatic sign-in fill
    on page load (`passwords.autoFill = false` by default); and a concentric borderless fill panel with a locked-vault
    **Unlock Passwords…** row (`passwords.offerUnlock = true` by default).
- **Workspace, web, sidebar, media & Markdown.**
  - **Notifications**: clicking a web notification from a Favorite or Pinned tab switches to the right window, native
    macOS window tab, and Space, and focuses the kept tab directly.
  - **In-place splits on Favorites & Pinned**: dropping a tab onto the middle of a Favorite row or Pinned tile (or onto
    the pane area while viewing one) splits it in place without releasing the kept tab to Today's tabs.
  - **Web context menus**: streamlined link menu (`Open Link in New Tab` plus an **Open Link In** submenu for Split
    Right/Down, New Window, and Private Tab), page-background Back/Forward/Copy Page Address/Print actions, and full
    WebKit Writing Tools, Live Text, image controls, QR detection, and Services integration.
  - **Now Playing**: ignores short notification chimes (`< 5 s` without `MediaSession` metadata) and gates Web Audio /
    sub-frame sound behind 3 s of sustained playback so chat pings do not flash media cards.
  - **Floating sidebar & window sharing**: keeps the floating sidebar open across its menus, popovers, extension popups,
    and tab hover previews; blurs strictly within-window content (`withinWindow`) on both material and Liquid Glass; and
    keeps window-sharing traffic lights inside the window frame so macOS's screen-sharing pill never clips.
  - **Documents & storage**: **Format** menu for inline/block Markdown editing and undoable table insertion (scoped to
    the key window's document), plus sealing of WebKit `WebExtensions`, `Origins`, `IsolatedSites`,
    `AlternativeServices`, and `EnhancedSecurity` storage directories at quit.
- **Sunset WebKit updated to `c308255c50`** (from `944f9ff672`) with all 37 Sunset patches rebased and verified against
  `TestWebKitAPI` and WebKit layout tests.

**Two variants of one app.**
- **Sunset** (`Sunset-v0.1.0-alpha.18-…`): pages on the system's WebKit, the one Safari uses.
- **Sunset with its own WebKit** (`Sunset-WebKit-v0.1.0-alpha.18-…`): the same app with WebKit's main branch at
  `c308255c50` and 37 patches of ours. Switch between them in Settings › General › Updates › Web engine.

**Install.** Unzip, move the app to Applications, open it. Both are signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple.
They need macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Check the download.** `shasum -a 256 -c NAME.zip.sha256` in the folder holding both files.

**Not in it yet.** Linux; Intel Macs; syncing the vault between Macs, KDBX / 1PUX imports; protected video on some
sites; several pieces of the roadmap (`docs/roadmap`, each entry says what is not done).

Report what breaks in the repository's issues.
