The tenth alpha of Sunset, in two builds. Expect rough edges, and settings or saved state that a later build may not read.

**Two builds.**
- **Sunset** (`Sunset-v0.1.0-alpha.10-…`): pages run on the system's WebKit, the one Safari uses, and get its security
  updates with macOS. Most people want this one.
- **Sunset WebKit** (`Sunset-WebKit-v0.1.0-alpha.10-…`, experimental): the same app with its own WebKit inside, built from
  WebKit's main branch at 944f9ff672 with 36 patches of ours. It gets **no WebKit security fixes from macOS updates**, only
  from a later Sunset WebKit; it says when its engine is eight weeks old.

**What changed since alpha.9.**
- **Images in the terminal.** Programs that show pictures with kitty's graphics protocol (`kitten icat`, yazi, chafa,
  timg) show them in Sunset: PNG, JPEG (sent compressed) and raw pixels; above or below the text; scaled to the cells asked;
  cropped; moving with the text as it scrolls; each screen its own. Only pictures sent in the escape are read, never a file
  a program names; a terminal keeps at most 64 MiB of them.
- **Security fixes** from an outside audit:
  - a site's icon and a notification's picture are fetched only from the public internet or the page's own host, through
    the page's proxy (a page could make Sunset reach your Mac or your network);
  - downloads are quarantined before they get their name;
  - a page can no longer keep its own data by faking a sign-in, nor end a screen share it did not start;
  - Sunset WebKit's loader settings no longer reach the programs of your terminals;
  - a vault file cannot make unlocking hang by naming a huge cost.
- **Passwords.** A new vault needs a master password of 12 characters or more; after three wrong ones, each try waits a
  little longer (up to 30 s). A signed release keeps its storage key with the Secure Enclave, or says it cannot.
- **Terminal links to files.** A Cmd-click on a link to an app, a script or an installer shows it in the Finder instead of
  running it (Settings ▸ Privacy and security ▸ Links to files).
- **Settings.** Appearance, Window and Tabs and sidebar are one section, Appearance and layout. The title bar's settings are
  gone (no title bar, the window's buttons with the sidebar); the sidebar's width and visibility are set from the window;
  the profile new windows open on is chosen from a menu.
- **Media.** Now Playing follows a page's mute at once; its cards have an edge and turn one at a time; picture in picture
  reaches players inside frames (Fox News); a new setting floats a playing video when you leave its tab.
- **Extensions in private windows** say why they do nothing there, and are allowed in one click.
- **Private windows** start with no favorites and no pinned tabs.
- **History** shows the sites' icons; a tab's colour follows the icon it shows.
- **Buttons** answer the pointer the same way everywhere; a copy is confirmed the same way everywhere; a long press, or a
  deep press on a Force Touch trackpad, opens a row's menu.
- **The welcome** plays a short opening, in time with its animation.

**Install.** Unzip, move the app to Applications, open it. Both are signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so
they open without a warning. They need macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Check the download.** `shasum -a 256 -c NAME.zip.sha256` in the folder holding both files.

**Not in it yet.** Linux; Intel Macs; updates (download the next build by hand); Sixel and iTerm2's images, kitty's
animations and Unicode placeholders; syncing the vault between Macs, importing from other password managers; protected
video on some sites (CNN says its DRM is not supported: not found yet); several pieces of the roadmap (`docs/roadmap`, each
entry says what is not done).

Report what breaks in the repository's issues.
