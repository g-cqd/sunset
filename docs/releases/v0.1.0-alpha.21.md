The twenty-first alpha of Sunset: Restart to Update no longer hangs; the window blur's edges are no longer nearly clear;
and fixes from the performance and security audit. Expect rough edges, and settings or saved state that a later build may
not read.

**What changed since alpha.20.**
- **Restart to Update** no longer waits on a question nobody sees: when quitting asks about what would be lost, the
  question goes on a window on screen, brought to the front. Before, it could be put on a window that was not shown, and
  Sunset waited for its answer, the update not installed.
- **Window blur**: its edges are as dense as its middle. The window's borders came out nearly clear, most of all over Clear
  glass.
- **Performance and memory.**
  - A page's picture for its tab's preview is preview-sized, not the page's whole size, unless another window shows it.
  - The terminal's shaders are compiled once, as Sunset starts and off the main thread; every terminal shares one command
    queue.
  - Content blocking's first compile lets go of each part as it is compiled.
- **Security.**
  - Notifications and screen sharing are asked for only right after a click or a key Sunset saw itself; what the page says
    of it is not taken. A frame in a page asking to share the screen is named.
  - The storage key is sealed bound to its own record; a key kept before is sealed again that way the first time it is
    read. The blocking lists' folder is only your user's.
- **Code scanning**: no alert left open; the web tester's local server reads and writes only inside its folders.

**Two variants of one app.**
- **Sunset** (`Sunset-v0.1.0-alpha.21-…`): pages on the system's WebKit, the one Safari uses.
- **Sunset with its own WebKit** (`Sunset-WebKit-v0.1.0-alpha.21-…`): the same app with WebKit's main branch at
  `c308255c50` and 37 patches of ours. Switch between them in Settings › General › Updates › Web engine.

**Install.** Unzip, move the app to Applications, open it. Both are signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple.
They need macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Check the download.** `shasum -a 256 -c NAME.zip.sha256` in the folder holding both files.

**Updating from alpha.20 or earlier.** Their Restart to Update can still hang: if Sunset does not quit after Restart,
quit it yourself; the update is installed as it quits.

**Not in it yet.** Linux; Intel Macs; two-key shortcut sequences; syncing the vault between Macs; KDBX / 1PUX imports;
protected video on some sites.

Report what breaks in the repository's issues.
