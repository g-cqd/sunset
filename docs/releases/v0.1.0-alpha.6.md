The sixth alpha of Sunset, in two builds. Expect rough edges, and settings or saved state that a later build may not read.

**Two builds.**
- **Sunset** (`Sunset-v0.1.0-alpha.6-…`): pages run on the system's WebKit, the one Safari uses, and get its security updates
  with macOS. Most people want this one.
- **Sunset WebKit** (`Sunset-WebKit-v0.1.0-alpha.6-…`, experimental): the same app with its own WebKit inside, now built from
  WebKit's main branch at 944f9ff672 with 36 patches of ours (browser extensions written for Firefox and Chrome, uBlock
  Origin among them, and pages that were orders of magnitude slower than in other browsers). Read before using it:
  - it gets **no WebKit security fixes from macOS updates**, only from a later Sunset WebKit;
  - to load its own WebKit it runs with a loader exception (`allow-dyld-environment-variables`) that the plain build does
    not have, and without the private entitlements Safari's WebKit has for its sandbox; its threat model is not written yet;
  - it is a separate app (`studio.aemi.sunset.webkit`) with its own settings, beside Sunset if both are installed.

**What changed since alpha.5.**
- **A welcome at the first launch.** The sun rises out of nothing, sets, and the window comes into focus around it (a click or
  a key skips it; with Reduce Motion it does not play). One question a page: what you will use (terminals, web, documents),
  how careful the web should be (Balanced, Strict, Compatible), whether Sunset opens your links, which search engine, what
  opens when there is no tab, and importing from another browser. Every answer stays in Settings. Help ▸ Welcome to Sunset
  shows it again; there may be another way to find it.
- **Import from Another Browser** (File menu): bookmarks from Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera, Chromium,
  Firefox, Zen, LibreWolf and Waterfox, or from an exported bookmarks file, into a new Space or this one's Favorites. Their
  extensions are listed with their store pages, for you to install. Safari's and Firefox's data need Full Disk Access; the
  window says so and opens the setting.
- **Tabs of every kind.** ⌘⇧T offers a terminal, a web page or a document; a setting chooses what ⌘T makes and what opens
  when a window has no tab.
- **Sharing a tab with a page** shows a picture of each tab, terminals included.
- **Questions in Sunset's look**, with Cancel the default where nothing should happen by accident. Fixed on the way: deleting
  a Space with tabs did nothing whichever button was pressed.
- Folders fold and unfold in motion.
- Sunset WebKit moved to a newer WebKit (aa9b09cf67 to 944f9ff672); a crash fix we carried is now upstream's.

**Install.** Unzip, move the app to Applications, open it. Both are signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so
they open without a warning. They need macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Check the download.** `shasum -a 256 -c NAME.zip.sha256` in the folder holding both files.

**Not in it yet.** Linux; Intel Macs; updates (download the next build by hand); Firefox containers made into Sunset profiles
(they are listed when importing, not made); in Sunset WebKit, extensions changing request headers, or the headers of images,
scripts and other subresources (pages and frames only); several pieces of the roadmap (`docs/roadmap`, each entry says what
is not done).

Report what breaks in the repository's issues.
