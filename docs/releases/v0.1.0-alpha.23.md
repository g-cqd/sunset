- **Light and dark**: the window background, tint, blur, accent and terminal colors each have a value in light and one in
  dark, macOS's own until you change them in that scheme. Your current settings are kept.
- **Window background**: Color, Material or Glass, each tinted; Color with no tint is the window's own color. The blur is
  offered with all three.
- **Scheme**: Sunset follows the system by default. When you force one, "Content follows" makes pages use it too.
- **Splits**: what Split Right and Split Down make is a setting (Tabs › New tabs).
