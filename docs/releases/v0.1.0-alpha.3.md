The third alpha of Sunset. Expect rough edges, and settings or saved state that a later build may not read.

**What changed since alpha.2.**
- Pop-ups a page sizes (sign-in, payment, share dialogs) open in a window of their own: no sidebar, the window's margin and
  material, "Open in ‹Space›" to make them a tab. From a private page they stay private. Settings ▸ Web ▸ Pop-up windows.
- Links from other apps open in a peek window with back, forward and an address field; "Open in ‹Space›" keeps them.
  Settings ▸ Web ▸ Links from other apps.
- Pages a page opens (a link to a new tab, a pop-up after a click) are shown, not left behind.
- The page menus work: Open Image or Video in New Tab, Download Image, Download Video, the link's Open in Split, New Tab,
  New Window and Private Tab, Search with your engine (not Safari).
- Plain-http sites: with HTTPS first, a site with no HTTPS shows Load over HTTP, which now loads; this Mac and the local network
  are never upgraded.
- Menus keep their less used items behind Option: Force Reload, Close Other Tabs (⌥⌘W, new), Open in New Tab, and more.
- With the sidebar floating, its field holds the page's address, as when docked.
- Popovers are opaque and readable over any page; the site settings popover has one text size and aligned rows.
- Settings reworded as System Settings words them; "Fall back to HTTP" is folded into "Secure connections".

**Install.** Unzip, move Sunset.app to Applications, open it. It is signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so it
opens without a warning. It needs macOS 26 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

**Check the download.** `shasum -a 256 -c Sunset-v0.1.0-alpha.3-build631-macos-arm64.zip.sha256` in the folder holding both
files.

**Not in it yet.** Linux; Intel Macs; updates (download the next build by hand); several pieces of the roadmap (`docs/roadmap`,
each entry says what is not done).

Report what breaks in the repository's issues.
