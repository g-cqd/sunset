## C — a notification's actions, requireInteraction, silent

Read: the Notifications standard (notifications.spec.whatwg.org, the source of 2026-11-17): **actions are for a service
worker's notifications only**; `new Notification` with actions throws a TypeError, and so does `renotify` without a tag.
So for the notifications a page can make, there are no actions to pass on: what was "not done" is not to be done.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Actions and renotify without a tag throw TypeError; `silent`, `requireInteraction`, `renotify` are read back; `silent` reaches Sunset | `WebNotificationsStandardTests`, a real page | Green. Red seen only as the test not building (no `silent`): the script's checks were written with it |

In the app, a silent notification makes no sound. `requireInteraction` has no per-notification switch on macOS (banner
or alert is the person's choice in System Settings): read back to the page, not acted on.
