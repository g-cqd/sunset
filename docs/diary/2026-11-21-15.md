# 2026-11-21 — 15: the welcome (roadmap 0111 H, 0084 G)

The owner asked for an onboarding at first launch (note n75), then, seeing it: an opening that comes from the sun, blurred
and not too fast; no "expanding wide then back"; no purple around it; "avoid faking stuff" (a drawn stand-in for the window
with fake corners); the sun and the sky in phases of their own; the window carved in by fade and blur, not shape; no blocks;
the presentation researched (docs/research/welcome-design.md); the window shorter; the main window held until the welcome
is passed; the search engine asked.
