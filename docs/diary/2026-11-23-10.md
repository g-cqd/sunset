# 2026-11-23 — 10: kitty's Unicode placeholders (roadmap 0010 E)

ask draws pictures with kitty's Unicode placeholders, not at the cursor, so Sunset showed none: ask never asked (it trusts
kitty and Ghostty only), and Sunset could not draw them. An image given a virtual placement (`U=1`) is drawn by the cells holding
`U+10EEEE` in the colour naming it, their marks saying row and column.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A virtual placement keeps the cursor and shows nothing alone | `KittyPlaceholderTests` | holds |
| ask's cells, both marks on each, draw the image once, scaled to its cells | the same, rows of cells as ask writes them | holds; one placement, 20 px into 4 × 2 cells of 10 × 20 |
| The image is fitted, proportions kept, centred, as kitty draws it | a 40 × 20 image in 4 × 4 cells | 40 × 20 pixels, 30 down: the rows above and below empty |
| Marks left out continue the cell before | kitty's short form against ask's full one | the same placements |
| The part drawn stays while the image scrolls out of the lines asked | lines asked from the second row; after scrolling | the same part, its first row read from the history |
| A cell written over splits the image | `x` over one cell | three parts, each the right source rectangle |
| A palette colour names an image, the third mark its high byte; a delete takes the virtual placement | the same | holds |
| Sunset's marks are ask's | `PlaceholderDiacriticsTests` against TerminalKit's table, all 297 | holds |
| The renderer draws them, with no glyph for `U+10EEEE` | `CPURendererPlaceholderTests` | holds; red without the glyph rule (48) and without the placements (133) |
| Each part of the core can fail | five mutants (`U=1` ignored, rows not followed down or up, no centring, marks not continued) | each red, 1 to 17 issues |
| kitty's own client draws right | `sunset-render … kitten icat --unicode-placeholder --place 16x4@2x1` on a four-colour 64 × 32 PNG | the quadrants in order, 64 × 32 in 8 × 2 cells of 8 × 17, at column 6, centred 1 px down |
