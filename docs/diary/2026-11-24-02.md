# 2026-11-24 — 02: A page out of every window crashed Sunset by focusing a sign-in field (n144)

Build 832 (the WebKit variant) quit on `NSInvalidArgumentException` from `-[NSPopover showRelativeToRect:ofView:
preferredEdge:]`, called by `AppPasswordFill.loginField(focused:in:)` on the script's message. The report and the
system log redact the reason.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A bad rect makes NSPopover raise (the rect is the page's layout, times the zoom) | a small AppKit program, one process per case: NaN, infinite, 1e300, outside the view, zero, negative | refuted: none raises |
| A view with no window does | the same program, the view not in a window | holds: `NSInvalidArgumentException`, "view has no window. You must supply a view in a window." |
| A page out of every window (a background tab, one being closed) has its field's focus told | `PasswordFillTests` "a field focused by a page out of every window is not told" (red first: `["focus username", "focus password"]`) | held; now refused: only on-screen pages are told (`PasswordFill.isOnScreen`), and the popover shows nothing for a view without a window or hidden (the save offer's path too) |

Not done: the save offer for a sign-in a page sends while out of every window is dropped, not kept for when the tab
comes back. Not seen live: the crash was not reproduced in the app (no background tab driven); the AppKit probe and
the test stand for it.
