# 2026-11-18 — 02: hidden pages let go of their memory (S-f; study t134)

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| WebKit's `inactiveSchedulingPolicy` puts a page out of its window to sleep, as a setting could use | `Scripts/probes/webkit-hidden-page-timers.swift`: a 50 ms timer in a window, out of it 3 s then 20 s, back | Refuted as a lever: ~19 ticks a second in a window, ~1 a second out of it with `.suspend`, `.throttle` and `.none` alike; full rate at once when back. The default is `.suspend` (raw 0). So a hidden page already sleeps, at once (docs/research/webkit-sleeping-pages-2026-11-18.md) |
| Memory pressure can be read without dispatch | `sysctl kern.memorystatus_vm_pressure_level` | Reads 1 (normal) here; `sysctlbyname` in `MemoryPressure` |
| Pages hidden longer than the delay go; under pressure the longest idle, one at a time; exempt ones never; 0 never by time | `PageSleepTests` (`PageSleep.choose`) | Red, observed (did not build); green |
| A page unloaded lets its view go, keeps its address, history and title, comes back when shown; one on screen is not unloaded | `PageSleepTests` (a WebKit page in a window) | Red (same); green |
| `web.unloadAfter` (12 h, 0–168) and `web.unloadUnderPressure` (on) read, written, out of range said, in Settings > Web > Memory | `ConfigurationPageSleepTests` | Red, observed; green. A first name, "Unload hidden pages after", was a fragment (rule 1 of docs/design/0005): "Unload delay for hidden pages" |
| An unloaded page's icon is dimmed | `TabIcon` reads `PaneContent.isUnloaded` | Not seen |
| The app unloads once a minute what the rules choose, not a page with text typed and not sent | `AppPageSleep` (Spaces, private windows' Spaces, Favorites; not the Pinned grids) | Not run on a live app: the delay is hours, and pressure cannot be made here on demand |

Full suite: 1,486 tests pass. Decision: ADR-0071 (no sleep setting, no moon; unload after a delay and under pressure).
Not measured: memory freed by an unload, time to come back.
