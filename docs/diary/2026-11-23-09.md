The owner (n138): "i suspect passwords to not be auto saving when adding a card there".

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A new card is kept only by Done | read: `PasswordsActions` keeps the new entry empty, opens it to be edited; `PasswordEntryPage` kept the draft only in `done()` | holds: filled in, then another entry or tab chosen (or the vault locked), the card stayed as it was made, empty |
| Edits can be kept as they are made, Cancel still going back | `EntryDraftTests` (red first: no `cancelEditing`): `beginEditing`, kept twice, `cancelEditing` gives the entry as editing began to keep again, then nothing | holds |

Then the owner: "the saving should be automatic and polled or react to recent changes or loss of focus".

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| An edit not yet kept is kept before the vault locks, by hand or idle | `PasswordsPendingTests` (red first: no `noteEdit`): an edit noted, then `lock()`, or 600 s of the test clock | holds: unlocked again, the card has its title. `VaultLock.willLock` is told before the key is wiped, for every reason |
| Noted edits are kept when Sunset loses focus | the same suite: `keepPending` | holds; the app delegate calls it when another app comes to the front |
| The page reacts to its window losing focus | `PasswordEntryPage`: `appearsActive` turning false keeps the draft | builds; not seen |

So an edit is kept: 0.7 s after the last change; when the window or the app loses focus; when the page goes away; and
before the vault locks, whatever the reason. Not a poll: each of those is an event.
