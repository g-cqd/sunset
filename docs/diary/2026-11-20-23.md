|---|---|---|
| A copy of Sunset with WebKit's frameworks and a launcher setting `DYLD_FRAMEWORK_PATH` runs on ours | ad hoc, unhardened variant; `lsof` on its processes | half: Sunset's process on ours; every page "stopped": WebKit's services not found (`bootstrap look-up … No such process`), the system's started instead, the GPU process "crashed" (the two WebKits do not speak) |
