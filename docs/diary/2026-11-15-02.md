# 2026-11-15 (2): pane backgrounds, the Library's corner, the top of a section

The owner, with the release of `4c0626f` in hand: "background for documents and all other panes should use the same background
we use for terminals screens / library button does not have concentric corners to the window edges / documents sorting and
adding buttons overlap with the search and the search makes no sense with no documents existing". Task t272.

Seen in a test copy of the debug app (`axdrive`, its own session and settings; nothing of the owner's screen), pixels read from
its window's picture at 2x.

## Every pane on the terminal's background (ADR-0037)

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| The panes that are not terminals have backgrounds of their own | Sources read | **Confirmed**: a document `textBackgroundColor`, the settings and an empty web pane `windowBackgroundColor`, each clear over a window that is not opaque |
| One value in the pane's context, drawn under every such pane | `PaneBackdropTests` written first | **Red first** (no `PaneBackdrop`, no `backdrop` in the context). Then green: the theme's colour, black with none or a wrong one; the scheme by its luminance; a view drawn on it has its colour and its alpha |
| In the app a document is then the terminal's colour | Pixels of a document, a terminal and the settings | **No, at first**: with `terminal.opacity` 0.2 the terminal was 27 and the document 35, over the window's 44. The terminal has its background twice (its layer's colour and its frame's pixels). Test added (red), alpha made `1 - (1 - a)²` |
| After that | The same, five configurations | Default: 27 / 27 / 27 (document / terminal / settings). `#0a1478`: (33, 39, 80) / (35, 39, 79) / (33, 39, 80). `#fdf6e3` at opacity 0.6: (224, 220, 208) / (224, 221, 208) / the same. Opaque `#0a1478`: (14, 36, 134) for the three. Within 2 of 255 on one channel |
| A light background has dark text in a dark window | Pictures of the document and the settings on `#fdf6e3` | Readable: the pane's scheme follows the background |
| An empty web pane | New Web Page, the address field left | The terminal's colour (15, 15, 15), the globe on it |

Not seen: a rendered Markdown document with code blocks and tables on a coloured theme (the kit's own tints over it), a page
that failed to load, an unknown kind of pane.

## Found on the way: the zoom button left at the top left by a new title

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| The three window buttons stay where they are put when the tab shown changes | Their places by Accessibility after Cmd-2, Cmd-1, a new document, sidebar on the right | **No**: the zoom button went to AppKit's place, the top left, under the content; close and minimise stayed |
| Why | A probe of 40 lines (a bare window, the three buttons moved, each one's frame watched, the title changed) with the stack of the call that did not take | AppKit drops `setFrameOrigin` on a button while that button says it moved (`-[_NSThemeWidget setFrameOrigin:ignoreRentry:]`). Each button was put back while the next said it had moved; the last one, never |
| A button that moves is put back again once AppKit is done | `WindowLightsTitleTests` written first: four titles, a file named for the title bar | **Red first** (the zoom button at 9 pt from the left after each title). Green with the window's title watched (a pass at once when AppKit is done with it) and a pass on the run loop's next turn after any move |
| In the app | The same places, sidebar right and left | The same before and after every change of tab and a new document |

This is the way ADR-0036 decided, made to hold; the decision is not changed.

## The Library's button and the window's corner

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| The owner's: its corner is not concentric with the window's | The measures read | **Confirmed**, and it could not be: 6.5 pt from the window's side (the margin, the tabs' inset, half a hairline) and 4.5 from its bottom (flush with the card), with the 5 pt corner of every sidebar button; a shared centre needs the two distances equal |

| So built | `SidebarCornerTests` written first | **Red first** (10 compile errors). Green: the bar 6.5 from the bottom docked, that corner 16 - 6.5 = 9.5 and never under 5, floating unchanged; drawn, only the named corner is cut deeper, by 7 to 14 px at 8 px a point |

The way back at the rail's foot, and the downloads' button when the Library's is off, have it too |

Not seen: the button under the pointer or pressed in the app (neither can be made from here); the picture is the same view
drawn off screen.

## The top of a section

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| The owner's: the sort and + buttons are over the search field | The measures read | **Confirmed**: in the Library the title's row was the close button's size and the tabs' spacing, shorter than the buttons in it, which were centred and so reached into the field |
| The row is as tall as its buttons; no field with nothing to search | `SectionTopTests` written first, the heading drawn with its buttons red and its field white | **Red first** (no `SectionTop`, no `SectionHeading`, no `isSearched`). Green: the buttons end above the field by the tabs' spacing, docked and floating, under the window's buttons and on their row; without a field the list starts higher by the field and its spacing |
| In the app | The Library on Documents with none, then one made with +; History with none | No field and "No documents yet" under the title; with one document the field is there, under the buttons with space between; History with none has no field |

What is searched for is forgotten when the field goes (the last document deleted while searching). Downloads and History
follow the same rule; the settings always have their field.
