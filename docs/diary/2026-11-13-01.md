# 2026-11-13: switching tabs (t205)

Harness: `SUNSET_PROBE_SWITCH=file` (`SwitchProbe`), release build, the app in the background. It selects every tab in turn, three
rounds; per switch: the time `select(item:)` took and the time until the main run loop was next idle. Rounds 1 and 2 (round 0
creates each content). Sessions: 12 terminals; 40 tabs, one terminal in four, the rest a local page.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `select(item:)` itself is slow | its own time | No: median 0.12 ms |
| A switch stalls the main thread for more than a frame | time to idle, 40 mixed tabs | Yes: median 30 ms, p90 39 ms (12 terminals: 22 ms, p90 29 ms) |
| The docked sidebar is most of it | the same with `sidebar.autoHide` (no sidebar in the tree) | Yes: median 11 to 15 ms |
| The rows are most of the sidebar's share | the sidebar with no rows | Half: 19 ms without them |
| Its fade mask or its blur bands are the rest | without the mask, without the bands, without both | No: 28 to 30 ms each |
| Rows that are equal views skip the work | `TabSidebarRow`, `Equatable`, `.equatable()` | No gain: 30.1 ms (stashed: `git stash`, "t205 rows") |
| The built-in tab bar is cheaper | `tabs.mode` internal | No: median 45 ms, p90 115 ms |
| Blur under the bars or the window's glass cost a switch | `barBlur` none; `window.material` opaque | No: 37 ms; 32 ms |

A sample of the main thread over two rounds (2.5 s busy of 15 s): SwiftUI's graph update about 19 %, `NSHostingView.layout` and
its display list about 40 %, of which a page's view moved into the window (`WebViewImpl::viewDidMoveToWindow`, the activity state
sent to its process) about 5 %; Core Animation's commit waiting on the render server about 8 %. Our own frames are under 3 %.

Not done: which SwiftUI views are updated (needs Instruments' SwiftUI template, or a build with `_printChanges`); frames on
screen (the window was in the background); the first frame of a terminal after it is shown; closing and focusing.
