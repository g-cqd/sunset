## Memory and idle cost (owner notes n11 and "memory and cpu usage appears to be very big")

Measured with the competitor harness (`bench/idle.sh sunset`, now with a Sunset case and an app option `--scrollback`; 100 000 lines of history like the others). First numbers, 0 lines: 118 MB, 486 M instructions/s, 62 wakeups/s; after 1M lines: 926 MB (peak 942), 1660 M instructions/s. The others: 63-134 MB at 0 lines, 0.04-5.7 M instructions/s idle, 0.3-15 wakeups/s; 81-470 MB after 1M lines.

Causes found by sampling, `vmmap` and a benchmark (not by guessing):
1. **Snapshots copied the whole history.** `takeSnapshot` returns a copy of the screen; the history was one flat array, so after each snapshot the next written line copied it (up to 100 000 lines). Benchmark `terminal/flood with a snapshot per read`: 2387 M instructions and 385 ms per MiB against 57 M for the same bytes with no snapshot (42x). Fix: the history is stored in chunks of 128 lines (`Scrollback`), a copy shares them, a write copies only the newest chunk, the oldest chunk is released when its lines are gone (storage is limit + one chunk) and recycled (the rolling `terminal/ascii` benchmark is back at 0 mallocs, 58 M). After: 75 M instructions, 12 ms per MiB (30x less), tests first (7 storage tests: order across wraps, bound, sharing).
2. **Every blink phase redrew the whole screen** (29 M instructions twice a second even at an idle prompt) because the text-blink phase flipped with the cursor phase. Now only rows that were drawn with blinking text are redrawn on a phase change (5 tests, first red: all rows were drawn).

Result (same harness, same machine, load 15): after 1M lines 291 MB (peak 307), was 926 (peak 942). Idle: 337 M instructions/s (was 486), 39 wakeups/s (was 62). Idle is still 60-100x the other terminals: what is left is presenting (each blink makes the whole 2460x1650 image: a 16 MB byte array, a 16 MB `Data` copy, a CGImage and Core Graphics' colour conversion on the CPU, seen as vImage in the samples) and wakeups that I have not traced.

## Idle cost, second round (owner: "memory and cpu usage appears to be very big")

After the history fix the idle cost was still 60-100x the others (337 M instructions/s, 39 wakeups/s). Three more causes, each measured:
3. **Presenting**: every frame made a 16 MB byte array, a 16 MB `Data` copy, a `CGImage`, and Core Graphics converted the colours on the CPU (vImage in every idle sample). Now `IOSurfacePresenter` (SunsetRenderMac; seam `FramePresenter` in the platform-free renderer): three `IOSurface`s tagged sRGB take turns, each gets only the pixel rows it lacks (the renderer reports `changedPixelRows`: 5 tests; presenter: 5 tests incl. odd widths, rows missed by other buffers, resize), the layer shows the surface and the compositor converts on the GPU. Colour probe unchanged: worst error 3/255 (the sRGB tag is kept on the surface). Output checked in a window through two resizes.
4. **The reader woke 20 times a second** (a 50 ms `poll` timeout to notice cancellation). Now it waits with no timeout on the master and on a cancellation pipe (test red first: 12 wakeups in 600 ms, and a cancel took the rest of the timeout; now at most 1, cancel under 30 ms).
5. **The cursor blinked forever**: each blink is a frame. `BlinkSchedule` stops it after 15 s without input or output (3 tests); the next key or output starts it again. A setting for the timeout (or none) belongs in the config file.
